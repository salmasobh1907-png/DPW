Five roles, each sees only what their job needs

Level 1

Executive

  • Buy rates, lowest standard rate

  • Quote, PDF, Gmail / Outlook draft
Level 1

Sales

  • Quick price on sell rates
  • Never sees buy rates or margin
  • Offers sent to them, feedback won / lost
Level 2

Direct Manager

  • Own team only
  • Workload, SLA, cycle time
  • Forward / reassign with reason
  • Training requests
Level 3

Senior Management

  • Cockpit for the department

  • Approve / reject decisions
Level 4

Top Management

  • Whole company cockpit
  • Margin, win rate, TEU, forecast
  • Morning brief

Role names, not person names or job titles. In production each role is an Entra ID group set by IT.

How it works: plain rules on read-only data

Sources (read only)

  • Rate Bank on SharePoint
  • Shared mailboxes (M365 Graph)
  • Later: NPM, CARGOES, Finance FX

Demo today: dummy Rate Bank file, Gmail test box with one +address per mailbox.

→

Rules (no AI)

  • Lowest rate = standard dry rates only; special, spot, DG, named customer never count
  • No validity date = valid to month end; spot = that sailing only
  • Sokhna: DP World terminal first, other terminal flagged
  • Jebel Ali: suspended carriers hidden
  • Profit on ocean freight only; THC and local charges at cost
  • Rate moves of 5% or more flagged; free time, D&D, ACI and FX date checked
→

Outputs

  • Quote email and locked PDF with check code
  • Gmail / Outlook draft, a person sends
  • Cases, SLA, dashboards, Cockpit
Worked example, China dry export, 40':buy USD 1,800+ profit USD 50= sell USD 1,850THC at cost

Other lanes: no fixed profit. The advisor sets it from the gap to the other carriers and to the market level on the lane.

A day for the Executive

08:45
Morning brief and Today. Rates expiring this week, lanes that moved, cases waiting, one "Next up".
09:00
Pull the mailboxes. Each email becomes a case with a reference number and an SLA clock.
09:10
Price it. The request is read, lowest standard rate found, margin options shown with buy + profit = sell.
09:15
Before you send. Rate move, free time and D&D, ACI on imports, currency and FX date.
09:20
Draft and PDF. Saved as a draft; the Executive checks and presses Send.
16:45
End of day and hand-over. Open items handed to the back-up with a note.

A week for management

Daily
Direct Manager: team load, late cases, who is away, reassign with reason.
Weekly
Senior Management: Cockpit headline, top 3 pains with owner and fix, approve or reject decisions.
Monthly
Top Management: margin, win rate, TEU, forecast, customers going down.

Built-in control

  • The tool never sends email; a person sends every draft
  • Sales never see buy rates
  • Every quote has a number and a check code; Verify catches edited copies
  • Sources are read only; the Rate Bank is never written to
  • No passwords stored; carrier sites are opened by the person
