Who it serves

Five roles, each sees only what the job needs

Level 1

Executive

Cases with SLA, buy rates, buy + profit = sell, drafts and PDF

Level 1

Sales

Quick price on sell rates, offers, won and lost feedback. Never buy rates.

Level 2

Direct Manager

Own team: load, late cases, cycle time, reassign with reason

Level 3

Senior Management

Department Cockpit: top 3 pains with owner and fix, approve or reject

Level 4

Top Management

Company Cockpit: margin, win rate, TEU, forecast, morning brief

Roles, not names or job titles. In production each role is an Entra ID group set by IT.

This is why the product sells inside the company: every level gets something on day one, not only the people who quote.
